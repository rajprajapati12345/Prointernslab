FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Can I join more than one track at the same time?

Yes, if you can keep up with the tasks in each. We suggest starting with one track and adding another once you have settled in.

Are there any fees for the internship?

Fee details for each track are shared with you before you accept an offer, so there are no surprises.

When will I receive the offer letter?

Once your application is accepted, the offer letter is emailed to you. If you cannot find it, check your spam folder.

How do I submit my tasks?

Your offer email includes a submission link. You can share a code repository or a hosted demo, depending on the task.

What are the selection criteria?

We look at your interest in the track, your background, and your willingness to finish tasks on time. You do not need earlier internship experience.

When will I get my certificate?

After you complete the required tasks, we issue your certificate with a unique ID that you can check on the Verify page.
